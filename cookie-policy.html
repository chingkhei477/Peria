Cookie Policy

Which cookies Periva uses, and why partner stores use cookies to track your rewards.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites store in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels. This policy covers those used on periva.in and by partner stores when you shop through Periva.

Cookies on periva.in

The public pages of periva.in do not set advertising or analytics cookies, and our fonts and files are served from our own website, not from third parties.

When you sign in to your Periva account, we use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to protect your account. The Service cannot work without them, so they cannot be switched off.

If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

Cookies used to track your rewards

When you click through from Periva to a partner store, the store or its affiliate network sets its own cookies, or uses similar technology, to record that your visit came from Periva. This is how the store knows to pay commission for your order, which funds your points.

These cookies are controlled by the store or network, and their own cookie and privacy policies apply. If you block them, your order may not track and you may not earn points.

Managing cookies

You can view, block and delete cookies in your browser settings, and allow cookies for particular websites. To make sure your order is tracked, allow cookies for the store you are visiting and pause ad blockers or privacy extensions for that visit.
